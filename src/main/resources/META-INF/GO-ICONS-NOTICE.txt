go.svg, goMod.svg, goWork.svg, goTest.svg and their _dark variants are
small-size adaptations of that design, independently redrawn as SVG geometry
by open Goland contributors. Changes include the pixel-grid simplification,
light/dark palettes, reduced-size mascot and module/workspace/test badges.
These adaptations are supplied under CC BY 4.0 with credit to Renée French.
No JetBrains GoLand image resource or proprietary artwork was copied.

Complete licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Tool-window icons, the template icon and the fuzz-corpus text icon use the already bundled
open-source IntelliJ Platform AllIcons. Their existing Apache-2.0 notices
remain in the platform distribution; those resources are not copied here.
